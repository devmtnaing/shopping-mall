# Greybox layout

The greybox is the mall made of plain boxes. We use it to tune movement, the camera and navigation before any art exists (roadmap Phase 1).
It's generated by `pnpm greybox` from [`tools/greybox/layout.ts`](../tools/greybox/layout.ts), which writes:

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `client/public/assets/mall/greybox.glb` | Visual model: one mesh per material (~14 draw calls) |
| `client/public/assets/mall/greybox.collision.glb` | Collision triangles for the player and camera (≤ 5k tris) |
| `client/public/assets/mall/mall.meta.json` | Slots, seats, spawns, zones, escalators ([schema](../shared/src/meta.ts)) |

The Blender mall (Phase 2) replaces these files with the same names and formats. Nothing in the client changes.

## Conventions

- Metres, **Y up**. The entrance is at **z = 0** and the mall runs along **−Z**.
- **Yaw** is in radians around +Y. `yaw = 0` faces −Z (into the mall) and `yaw = π/2` faces −X.
  Forward is `(−sin yaw, 0, −cos yaw)`, the same as a Three.js object's default facing.
- **West** (−X) is on your left as you walk in. **East** (+X) is on your right.

## Zones

`Entrance`, `Main hall`, `Fountain court`, `Upper gallery`, `Sky court`, `Sky bridge`, and one per shop unit (priority 10).
The client shows the name of the highest-priority zone containing the player. Shop zones take their name from `mall.config.ts`.

## Movement tests built into the layout

- **Step-up:** flagship steps (0.2 m) must be walkable. Benches (0.45 m) must block.
- **Slopes:** escalator surfaces (~30–32°) must be walkable even when they aren't moving.
- **Ceilings:** walking under the sky bridge and jumping under the upper slab.
- **Tight spots:** 1.55 m between the planters at x ±6.5 and escalator A's balustrade.
- **Escalators:** the walking surface is an invisible 30° ramp level with the middle of the moving steps (drawn by the client, `client/src/world/escalators.ts`); invisible walls along the balustrades keep you on it.
