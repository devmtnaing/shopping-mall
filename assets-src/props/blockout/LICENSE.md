# Blockout props

`workstation.glb` and `course-screen.glb` are built from boxes by `tools/assets/software-props.ts`
(run `node tools/assets/software-props.ts` to rebuild them). They're part of the project and under
its asset licence (CC BY 4.0). They stand in until Higgsfield models replace them: the prompts are in
`assets-src/props/higgsfield/prompts.md`.
